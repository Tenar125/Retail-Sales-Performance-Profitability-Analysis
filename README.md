# Retail-Sales-Performance-Profitability-Analysis
End-to-end retail sales data analysis using Python, SQL, and Power BI.
Buisness problem :
The retail buisness records daily sales transactions but struggles to understand the customer behaviour and identify which product sales more or less .Due to the large number of transactions .It becomes difficult to understand analyze performance and make decisions manually.
Buisness Questions and Hypothesis :
1:Product Category vs Revenue
H₀: All categories contribute equally.
H₁: Some categories generate more revenue.
2:Price vs Sales Volume
H₀: Price has no effect on quantity sold.
H₁: Lower-priced products sell more.
3:Repeat vs One-time Customers
H₀: Repeat & one-time customers generate similar revenue.
H₁: Repeat customers generate higher revenue.
4:Sales by Month/Season
H₀: Sales are evenly distributed.
H₁: Sales peak in certain months/seasons.
5:Region vs Revenue
H₀: Revenue evenly distributed across regions.
H₁: Some regions generate more revenue
Key Insights:
Some product categories generate significantly more revenue than others.
Repeat customer generate significantly more revenue per customer compared to one-time buyers.
Sales peak in November,December and September
The West  Region generates the Highest revenue.
Tools Used :
Python (Data Cleaning & Preprocessing)
SQL (Exploratory & Business Queries)
Power BI (Interactive Dashboard)
